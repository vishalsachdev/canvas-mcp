Two things narrow it from the advisory's framing: the carrier is the MCP request's `_meta` dict, not an inbound HTTP header, and `TraceContextTextMapPropagator.extract` only reaches `TraceState.from_header` after a well-formed `traceparent` matches — so a caller has to supply both, which is trivial but does mean it is not a bare-header drive-by.
