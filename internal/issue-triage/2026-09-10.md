The correction: the brief offers "rewrite this branch's history before merging" as one of two
options. That will not work. Once a commit has been pushed to a public repo and associated with a
pull request, GitHub keeps it reachable by SHA through the `refs/pull/*` namespace, and a
force-push additionally leaves the superseded SHA visible in the PR's own timeline. Deleting the
branch does not remove it either. Rewriting history would give the *appearance* of remediation
while the report stayed fetchable — the worse of the two outcomes, because it would stop the clock
on the real fix. Treat the content as disclosed as of 2026-09-07.

3. **Optionally ask GitHub Support to purge** the cached commit view — that is the only mechanism

4. **Keep future review reports untracked.** `internal/` is already deny-by-default in
